PASTE_YOUR_DATABASE_ID_HERE

How to find it: open your Notion database as a full page, look at the URL:
https://www.notion.so/yourworkspace/DATABASE_ID?v=view_id
The DATABASE_ID is the 32-character string (no dashes needed) right after
your workspace name and before the "?v=".

If you're running /setup, it will ask you for this and write the real
notion-database-id.txt for you — you don't need to edit this file directly.
